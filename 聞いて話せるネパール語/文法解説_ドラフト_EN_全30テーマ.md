# Listen & Speak Nepali — Grammar Guide for Beginners (All 30 Themes · DRAFT · EN)

- Readings are **romanized** (approximate). Before shipping, check against the audio (Hemkala) and have a Nepali speaker review.
- Example sentences follow the in-app content. Explanations use standard forms.
- Symbols: ā/ī/ū = long vowels, ã/ũ/ẽ/õ = nasalized vowels.

---

## Theme 1「Present Tense (Affirmative)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "I am ~" and "I do ~".

### 1. Subject words
| Nepali | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| म | ma | I |
| उ | u | he |
| उनी | unī | she (polite) |
| यो | yo | this |
| हामी | hāmī | we |

### 2a. "to be" for nouns (A is B)
| Subject | "is/am" | Romanized |
|---|---|---|
| I म | हुँ | hũ |
| he / this उ／यो | हो | ho |

| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| म विद्यार्थी **हुँ**। | ma vidyārthī **hũ** | I am a student |
| उ डाक्टर **हो**। | u ḍākṭar **ho** | He is a doctor |
| यो किताब **हो**। | yo kitāb **ho** | This is a book |

### 2b. "do ~" (verb + छ)
| Subject | Ending | Example (eat खानु) | Romanized |
|---|---|---|---|
| I म | ~**छु** | खान्**छु** | khān**chu** |
| he उ | ~**छ** | खान्**छ** | khān**cha** |
| she उनी | ~**छिन्** | खान्**छिन्** | khān**chin** |
| we हामी | ~**छौं** | खान्**छौं** | khān**chaũ** |

| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| म भात **खान्छु**। | ma bhāt khānchu | I eat rice |
| उ पानी **पिउँछ**। | u pānī piũcha | He drinks water |
| उनी स्कूल **जान्छिन्**। | unī skūl jānchin | She goes to school |

### 3. Word order = Subject → Object → Verb
| | Subject | Object | Verb |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nepali | म (ma) | नेपाली (nepālī) | बोल्छु (bolchu) |
| English | I | Nepali | speak |

⚠️ Use **हो/हुँ** for "A is B" (nouns), and **verb + छ** for actions. They are different.

## Theme 2「Present Tense (Negative)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "I don't ~".

### 1. Key rule: there is no separate word for "not"
The affirmative **छ (cha)** changes to the negative **दैन／दिन (daina／dina)** family — the verb itself becomes negative.

### 2. Negative conjugation (eat खानु)
| Subject | Affirmative | Negative | Romanized |
|---|---|---|---|
| I म | खान्छु | **खाँदिनँ** | khā̃**dinã** |
| he उ | खान्छ | **खाँदैन** | khā̃**daina** |
| she उनी | खान्छिन् | **खाँदिनन्** | khā̃**dinan** |
| we हामी | खान्छौं | **खाँदैनौं** | khā̃**dainaũ** |

### 3. Examples (affirmative → negative)
| Affirmative | Negative | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| म पानी पिउँछु | म पानी **पिउँदिनँ** (piũdinã) | I don't drink water |
| उ आउँछ | उ **आउँदैन** (āũdaina) | He doesn't come |
| उनी स्कूल जान्छिन् | उनी स्कूल **जाँदिनन्** (jā̃dinan) | She doesn't go to school |

### 4. A special pattern: "to like"
| Part | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| मलाई | malāī | to me |
| मन पर्छ | man parcha | is liked (= like) |

| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| मलाई कफी मन **पर्दैनँ**। | malāī kaphī man **pardainã** | I don't like coffee |

⚠️ With "like", the subject is not "I म" but "**to me मलाई**". Literally: "to me coffee is not liked".

## Theme 3「Past Tense (Affirmative)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "I did ~". **The biggest hurdle, "ले (le)", appears here.**

### 1. Past-tense endings
| Subject | Ending | Example | Romanized |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | ~**एँ** | खा**एँ** (ate) | khā**ẽ** |
| he | ~**यो** | सुत्**यो** (slept) | sut**yo** |
| she | ~**इन्** | ग**इन्** (went) | ga**in** |
| we | ~**यौं** | हेर्**यौं** (watched) | her**yaũ** |

### 2.【Most important】the subject of a transitive verb takes "ले"
For verbs that act on an object — eat, read, write, do — add **ले** to the subject.
| Subject | +ले | Romanized |
|---|---|---|
| I म | **मैले** | maile |
| he उसले | **उसले** | usle |
| she उनले | **उनले** | unle |
| we हामीले | **हामीले** | hāmīle |

**Takes ले (transitive)**
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **मैले** किताब पढेँ। | maile kitāb paḍhẽ | I read a book |
| **उसले** हिजो काम गर्यो। | usle hijo kām garyo | He worked yesterday |

**No ले (intransitive: sleep, go)**
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| उ सुत्यो। | u sutyo | He slept |
| उनी स्कूल गइन्। | unī skūl gain | She went to school |

### 3. How to tell them apart
| Type | Examples | ले |
|---|---|---|
| acts on an object (transitive) | eat, read, write, do, watch | **takes ले** |
| just moves (intransitive) | sleep, go, come, sit | no ले |

⚠️ The #1 beginner mistake is **forgetting ले**. Remember: "action on an object = subject + ले".

## Theme 4「Past Tense (Negative)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "I didn't ~".

### 1. The negative past adds "न (na/n)"
| Subject | Past affirmative | Past negative | Romanized |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | खाएँ | **खाइनँ** | khā**inã** |
| he | आयो | **आएन** | ā**ena** |
| she | गइन् | **गइनन्** | ga**inan** |
| we | हेर्यौं | **हेरेनौँ** | here**naũ** |

### 2. Examples (affirmative → negative)
| Affirmative | Negative | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| मैले खाएँ | मैले **खाइनँ** | I didn't eat |
| उ आयो | उ **आएन** | He didn't come |
| म हिजो सुतेँ | म हिजो **सुतिनँ** (sutinã) | I didn't sleep yesterday |

### 3. "ले" does not disappear in the negative
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **उसले** त्यो किताब पढेन। | usle tyo kitāb paḍhena | He didn't read that book |

⚠️ Even in the negative, a transitive subject keeps **ले**. Don't drop it.

## Theme 5「Future Tense」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "I will ~". **You can reuse the Theme 1 form.**

### 1. Key rule: future = present form + a time word
### 2. Time words
| Nepali | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| भोलि | bholi | tomorrow |
| अर्को हप्ता | arko haptā | next week |
| भविष्यमा | bhaviṣyamā | in the future |
| आज राति | āja rāti | tonight |

### 3. How to build it = just add a time word
| Present | + time word → future | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| म स्कूल जान्छु | म **भोलि** स्कूल जान्छु | I'll go to school tomorrow |
| म डाक्टर बन्छु | म **भविष्यमा** डाक्टर बन्छु | I'll become a doctor in the future |

| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| उनी **अर्को हप्ता** काठमाडौं आउँछिन्। | unī arko haptā kāṭhmāḍaũ āũchin | She'll come to Kathmandu next week |
| उ **आज राति** साथीलाई फोन गर्छ। | u āja rāti sāthīlāī phon garcha | He'll call a friend tonight |

⚠️ The form is the same as Theme 1 — just place a "when" word near the front.

## Theme 6「Questions」

🎯 **Goal**: Ask "Is it ~?" and "what / where / how much …?".

### 1. Yes/No questions = same word order, just rise at the end
| Statement | Question | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| यो किताब हो। | यो किताब **हो?** | Is this a book? |
| तपाईं नेपाली **हुनुहुन्छ?** | tapāī̃ nepālī hunuhuncha? | Are you Nepali? |

※ **तपाईं (tapāī̃)** = polite "you"; **हुनुहुन्छ (hunuhuncha)** = polite "are/do".

### 2. Question words (keep them in place)
| Word | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| के | ke | what |
| कहाँ | kahā̃ | where |
| कति | kati | how much / many |
| कहिले | kahile | when |

| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| यो **के** हो? | yo ke ho? | What is this? |
| शौचालय **कहाँ** छ? | śaucālaya kahā̃ cha? | Where is the toilet? |
| यो **कति** पर्छ? | yo kati parcha? | How much does this cost? |
| बस **कहिले** आउँछ? | bas kahile āũcha? | When does the bus come? |

⚠️ Unlike English, the question word is **not moved to the front** — it stays in its natural place.

## Theme 7「Requests (please ~)」

🎯 **Goal**: Politely ask someone to "please ~".

### 1. Formula: verb root + **नुहोस् (-nuhos)**
| Verb (meaning) | Request form | Romanized |
|---|---|---|
| आउनु (come) | आउ**नुहोस्** | āu**nuhos** |
| बस्नु (sit) | बस्**नुहोस्** | bas**nuhos** |
| सुन्नु (listen) | सुन्**नुहोस्** | sun**nuhos** |
| हेर्नु (look) | हेर्**नुहोस्** | her**nuhos** |

### 2. Examples
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ढोका **खोल्नुहोस्**। | ḍhokā kholnuhos | Please open the door |
| यहाँ नाम **लेख्नुहोस्**। | yahā̃ nām lekhnuhos | Please write your name here |
| **पर्खनुहोस्**। | parkhanuhos | Please wait |

⚠️ Adding **नुहोस्** makes it polite — use it with elders and people you've just met.

## Theme 8「Imperatives (do ~)」

🎯 **Goal**: Give commands at the right politeness level.

### 1. Three politeness levels
| Listener | How to build | Example (get up उठ्नु) | Romanized | Nuance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| elder / polite | root + **नुहोस्** | उठ्**नुहोस्** | uṭh**nuhos** | please do ~ |
| equal / mid | root + **ऊ** | उठ (≒उठ्**नू**) | uṭh | do ~ |
| close / lower | root only | **उठ** | uṭh | do ~ (plain) |

### 2. Examples
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning | Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| यहाँ **आउनुहोस्**। | yahā̃ āunuhos | Come here | polite |
| चाँडो **खाऊ**। | cā̃ḍo khāū | Eat quickly | mid |
| ढोका बन्द **गर**। | ḍhokā banda gar | Close the door | plain |
| चाँडो **उठ**। | cā̃ḍo uṭh | Wake up quickly | plain |

※ **कृपया (kripayā) = please**. Adding it softens the command (e.g., कृपया चुप लाग्नुहोस् = please be quiet).

⚠️ Choose the level by your relationship. When in doubt, the polite **नुहोस्** is safe.

## Theme 9「Prohibition (you must not ~)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "you must not ~" and "please don't ~".

### 1. Two ways
| Way | How to build | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ① rule / prohibition | root + **नुहुँदैन** (-nuhũdaina) | must not ~ |
| ② polite "don't" | **न** + root + **नुहोस्** | please don't ~ |

### 2. Examples
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| यहाँ फोटो **खिच्नुहुँदैन**। | yahā̃ phoṭo khicnuhũdaina | You must not take photos here | ① |
| झूट **बोल्नुहुँदैन**। | jhūṭ bolnuhũdaina | You must not tell lies | ① |
| कृपया चुरोट **नपिउनुहोस्**। | kripayā curoṭ napiunuhos | Please don't smoke | ② |
| कृपया **नदौड्नुहोस्**। | kripayā nadauḍnuhos | Please don't run | ② |

⚠️ For ②, put **न (na)** in front of the verb root (न + दौड्नुहोस् → **न**दौड्नुहोस्).

## Theme 10「Exclamations (how ~!)」

🎯 **Goal**: Express feeling with "how ~!".

### 1. Formula: **कति (kati) + adjective + !**
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **कति** सुन्दर! | kati sundar! | How beautiful! |
| **कति** ठूलो घर! | kati ṭhūlo ghar! | What a big house! |
| **कति** छिटो! | kati chiṭo! | So fast! |

### 2. Emphasis words
| Word | Romanized | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| एकदमै | ekdamai | extremely | एकदमै मिठो! (So delicious!) |
| साँच्चै | sā̃ccai | really | साँच्चै! (Really!) |
| अद्भुत | adbhut | amazing | अद्भुत! (Amazing!) |

⚠️ In a question **कति** means "how much?", in an exclamation it means "how ~!". Tell them apart by the punctuation and tone.

## Theme 11「Progressive (~ing)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "I am ~ing right now".

### 1. Formula: root + **इरहेको／इरहेकी छ** (-iraheko/-irahekī cha)
| Subject | Form | Romanized |
|---|---|---|
| I म | ~इरहेको **छु** | -iraheko **chu** |
| he उ | ~इरहेको **छ** | -iraheko **cha** |
| she उ (f) | ~इरहे**की** छ | -irahe**kī** cha |

### 2. Examples
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| म अहिले **पढिरहेको छु**। | ma ahile paḍhiraheko chu | I am studying right now |
| बच्चा **रोइरहेको छ**। | baccā roiraheko cha | The baby is crying |
| उ **सुतिरहेकी छ**। | u sutirahekī cha | She is sleeping |

### 3. A shorter, colloquial form: root + **दैछ** (-daicha)
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| उ **खाँदैछ**। | u khā̃daicha | He is eating |
| म पानी **पिउँदैछु**। | ma pānī piũdaichu | I am drinking water |

⚠️ A female subject uses **-की (kī)**, a male subject **-को (ko)** — the ending changes by gender.

## Theme 12「Become / get ~ (change of state)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "become ~" and "got ~".

### 1. Two "become" verbs
| Verb | Romanized | Use |
|---|---|---|
| हुनु | hunu | qualities, weather, time "become ~" |
| बन्नु | bannu | roles/positions "become ~" (doctor, friend) |

### 2. Formula: adjective/noun + हुन्छ (become) / भयो (became)
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| चिसो **भयो**। | ciso bhayo | It got cold |
| न्यानो **हुन्छ**। | nyāno huncha | It becomes warm |
| चाँडै रात **हुन्छ**। | cā̃ḍai rāt huncha | Night falls soon |
| उहाँ डाक्टर **बन्नुभयो**। | uhā̃ ḍākṭar bannubhayo | He became a doctor (polite) |
| साथी **बन्न चाहन्छु**। | sāthī banna cāhanchu | I want to become a friend |

⚠️ "became" = **भयो (bhayo)**, "becomes" = **हुन्छ (huncha)**. For roles, use **बन्नु**.

## Theme 13「Using Adjectives」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "~ is (adjective)".

### 1. Formula: (subject) + adjective + **छ (cha)**
※ For qualities/states, "is" is **छ, not हो** (important).
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| यो फूल सुन्दर **छ**। | yo phūl sundar cha | This flower is beautiful |
| त्यो किताब पुरानो **छ**। | tyo kitāb purāno cha | That book is old |
| यो पानी चिसो **छ**। | yo pānī ciso cha | This water is cold |

### 2. Common adjectives
| Ne | Rom | Meaning | Ne | Rom | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| सानो | sāno | small | ठूलो | ṭhūlo | big |
| नयाँ | nayā̃ | new | पुरानो | purāno | old |
| अग्लो | aglo | tall | पिरो | piro | spicy |

### 3. It can also go before the noun
पुरानो किताब (purāno kitāb) = an old book

⚠️ "noun = noun" uses **हो** (Theme 1); "~ is (adjective)" uses **छ**. Don't mix them up.

## Theme 14「Using Adverbs」

🎯 **Goal**: Describe how an action is done ("fast", "slowly", "always").

### 1. Formula: put the adverb **before the verb**
| Adverb | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| छिटो | chiṭo | fast |
| बिस्तारै | bistārai | slowly |
| प्रायः | prāyaḥ | often / usually |
| सधैँ | sadhaĩ | always |
| राम्रोसँग | rāmrosã̄ga | well |

### 2. Examples
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ऊ धेरै **छिटो** दौड्छ। | ū dherai chiṭo dauḍcha | He runs very fast |
| म **बिस्तारै** हिँड्छु। | ma bistārai hĩḍchu | I walk slowly |
| ऊ **सधैँ** चाँडो उठ्छ। | ū sadhaĩ cā̃ḍo uṭhcha | He always wakes up early |
| ऊ **राम्रोसँग** नेपाली बोल्छे। | ū rāmrosã̄ga nepālī bolche | She speaks Nepali well |

※ **धेरै (dherai) = very** — it further strengthens an adverb.

⚠️ Adverbs usually go **right before the verb** (e.g., छिटो दौड्छ = run fast).

## Theme 15「Want to ~ (desire)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "I want to ~".

### 1. Two ways
| Way | Formula | Nuance |
|---|---|---|
| ① strong intent | root + **न चाहन्छु** (-na cāhanchu) | want to ~ |
| ② mood / preference | **मलाई** + root + **न मन छ** (-na man cha) | feel like ~ing |

### 2. Examples
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning | Way |
|---|---|---|---|
| म पानी **पिउन चाहन्छु**। | ma pānī piuna cāhanchu | I want to drink water | ① |
| म घर **जान चाहन्छु**। | ma ghar jāna cāhanchu | I want to go home | ① |
| मलाई संगीत **सुन्न मन छ**। | malāī saṅgīt sunna man cha | I feel like listening to music | ② |
| मलाई साथीलाई **भेट्न मन छ**। | malāī sāthīlāī bheṭna man cha | I feel like meeting a friend | ② |

⚠️ ① uses the subject "म", ② uses the subject "**मलाई (to me)**". ② is softer (mood/preference).

## Theme 16「Can ~ (ability)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "I can / can't ~".

### 1. Formula: root + **न सक्छु** (-na sakchu)
| Subject | Form | Romanized |
|---|---|---|
| I म | ~न **सक्छु** | sakchu |
| he उ | ~न **सक्छ** | sakcha |
| she उनी | ~न **सक्छिन्** | sakchin |
| negative (I) | ~न **सक्दिनँ** | sakdinã |

### 2. Examples
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| म नेपाली **बोल्न सक्छु**। | ma nepālī bolna sakchu | I can speak Nepali |
| उनी पियानो **बजाउन सक्छिन्**। | unī piyāno bajāuna sakchin | She can play the piano |
| म साइकल चलाउन **सक्दिनँ**। | ma sāikal calāuna sakdinã | I can't ride a bicycle |
| के तपाईं खाना पकाउन **सक्नुहुन्छ?** | ke tapāī̃ khānā pakāuna saknuhuncha? | Can you cook? (polite) |

⚠️ "can" = **सक्नु (saknu)**; negative = **सक्दिनँ**. The preceding verb takes root + **न**.

## Theme 17「Must ~ (obligation)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "I have to ~".

### 1. Formula: verb (dictionary form -नु) + **पर्छ (parcha)**
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| म **जानु पर्छ**। | ma jānu parcha | I have to go |
| म **सुत्नु पर्छ**。 | ma sutnu parcha | I have to sleep |

### 2. Transitive verbs take "ले" on the subject (review of Theme 3)
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **उसले** खानु पर्छ। | usle khānu parcha | He has to eat |
| **उसले** औषधि खानु पर्छ। | usle auṣadhi khānu parcha | He has to take medicine |
| **हामीले** पढ्नु पर्छ। | hāmīle paḍhnu parcha | We have to study |

⚠️ Even with "have to", a transitive subject takes **ले** (उसले／हामीले).

---

## Theme 18「May / allowed to ~ (permission)」

🎯 **Goal**: Ask "May I ~?".

### 1. Formula: root + **न मिल्छ？／न सकिन्छ？** (is it OK to ~?)
| Word | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| मिल्छ | milcha | is fine / OK |
| सकिन्छ | sakincha | is possible / allowed |

### 2. Examples
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **बस्न मिल्छ?** | basna milcha? | May I sit down? |
| यहाँ फोटो **खिच्न सकिन्छ?** | yahā̃ phoṭo khicna sakincha? | May I take a photo here? |
| झ्याल **खोल्न सकिन्छ?** | jhyāl kholna sakincha? | May I open the window? |
| तपाईं भित्र **आउन सक्नुहुन्छ**。 | tapāī̃ bhitra āuna saknuhuncha | You may come in (polite) |

⚠️ To ask permission, raise your tone into a question (मिल्छ? / सकिन्छ?).

---

## Theme 19「I think ~ (opinion / guess)」

🎯 **Goal**: State your view with "I think ~".

### 1. Formula: (the statement) + **जस्तो लाग्छ** (jasto lāgcha)
"It seems/I think ~". The trick: **attach it at the end of the sentence**.
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| भोलि पानी पर्छ **जस्तो लाग्छ**। | bholi pānī parcha jasto lāgcha | I think it will rain tomorrow |
| उ विद्यार्थी हो **जस्तो लाग्छ**। | u vidyārthī ho jasto lāgcha | I think he is a student |
| यो काम गाह्रो छ **जस्तो लाग्छ**。 | yo kām gāhro cha jasto lāgcha | I think this work is difficult |

⚠️ Unlike English "I think (that) …", in Nepali **the idea comes first, and "जस्तो लाग्छ" comes last** — the order is reversed.

---

## Theme 20「Might ~ (possibility)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "~ might happen".

### 1. Two ways
| Way | Formula | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ① from the verb | root + **न सक्छ** ／ **हुनसक्छ** | there is a chance ~ |
| ② with an adverb | **सायद (sāyad)** + sentence | perhaps ~ |

### 2. Examples
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| भोलि पानी **पर्न सक्छ**। | bholi pānī parna sakcha | It might rain tomorrow |
| यो गाह्रो **हुनसक्छ**। | yo gāhro hunasakcha | This might be difficult |
| **सायद** उहाँ आउनुहुन्न। | sāyad uhā̃ āunuhunna | Perhaps he won't come |
| रेल ढिलो **हुनसक्छ**। | rel ḍhilo hunasakcha | The train might be late |

⚠️ The form resembles Theme 16 "can (सक्छु)", but here **हुनसक्छ／न सक्छ** expresses "possibility".

---

## Theme 21「Have done ~ (experience)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "I have (ever) done ~".

### 1. Formula: root + **एको छु** (-eko chu) + ले on the subject (transitive)
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| मैले सुशी **खाएको छु**। | maile suśī khāeko chu | I have eaten sushi |
| मैले पोखरा **गएको छु**। | maile pokharā gaeko chu | I have been to Pokhara |
| मैले हिउँ **देखेको छु**। | maile hiũ dekheko chu | I have seen snow |
| मैले हवाईजहाज **चढेको छु**। | maile havāījahāj caḍheko chu | I have ridden in an airplane |

⚠️ The form resembles the progressive (Theme 11, "~इरहेको छु"), but this is **एको छु (experience)** — no "इ". Don't forget **ले** on the subject.

---

## Theme 22「Comparison (~ than)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "A is more ~ than B".

### 1. Formula: **B + भन्दा (bhandā) + adjective** (more ~ than B)
Attach **भन्दा** to the thing you compare against.
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| स्याउ **केराभन्दा** गुलियो छ। | syāu kerābhandā guliyo cha | Apples are sweeter than bananas |
| ऊ **मभन्दा** अग्लो छ। | ū mabhandā aglo cha | He is taller than me |
| आज **हिजोभन्दा** चिसो छ। | āja hijobhandā ciso cha | Today is colder than yesterday |
| **बसभन्दा** रेल छिटो छ। | basbhandā rel chiṭo cha | The train is faster than the bus |

⚠️ The adjective does not change (no "-er" form). Just add "the other thing + भन्दा".

---

## Theme 23「Conjunctions (and / but / so)」

🎯 **Goal**: Connect words and sentences.

### 1. Common conjunctions
| Conjunction | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| र | ra | and (list) |
| तर | tara | but |
| ~(भए)कोले | (bhae)kole | because / so |
| पनि…पनि | pani…pani | both … and |

### 2. Examples
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| मलाई चिया **र** कफी मन पर्छ। | malāī ciyā ra kaphī man parcha | I like tea and coffee |
| ऊ विद्यार्थी हो **तर** काम गर्छ। | ū vidyārthī ho tara kām garcha | He is a student but works |
| थकित **भएकोले**, मैले चाँडो सुतेँ। | thakit bhaekole, maile cā̃ḍo sutẽ | Since I was tired, I slept early |
| उनी गाउन **पनि** नाच्न **पनि** राम्री छिन्। | unī gāuna pani nācna pani rāmrī chin | She is good at both singing and dancing |

⚠️ "because" = **कोले／भएकोले** at the end of the reason clause. "both … and" = **पनि** twice.

---

## Theme 24「While ~ (simultaneous actions)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "~ while doing …".

### 1. Formula: verb 1 root + **दै (-dai)** + verb 2
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| संगीत **सुन्दै** पढ्छु। | saṅgīt sundai paḍhchu | I study while listening to music |
| **हिँड्दै** कुरा गरौँ। | hĩḍdai kurā garaũ | Let's talk while walking |
| टेलिभिजन **हेर्दै** खाना खान्छु। | ṭelibhijan herdai khānā khānchu | I eat while watching television |
| कफी **पिउँदै** अखबार पढ्छु। | kaphī piũdai akhbār paḍhchu | I read the newspaper while drinking coffee |

⚠️ Add **दै** to the "while" verb, and put the **main action last**.

---

## Theme 25「After ~ (sequence)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "after doing ~, …".

### 1. Formula: verb 1 root + **एपछि (-epachi)** + verb 2
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| हात **धोएपछि**, खान्छु। | hāt dhoepachi, khānchu | After washing my hands, I eat |
| **उठेपछि**, अनुहार धुन्छु। | uṭhepachi, anuhār dhuncha | After getting up, I wash my face |
| कफी **पिएपछि**, काम सुरु गर्छु। | kaphī piepachi, kām suru garchu | After drinking coffee, I start work |
| काठमाडौँ **आएपछि**, अङ्ग्रेजी पढ्न थालेँ। | kāṭhmāḍaũ āepachi, aṅgrejī paḍhna thālẽ | After coming to Kathmandu, I started studying English |

⚠️ Theme 24 "while (दै)" is simultaneous; this "after (एपछि)" is sequence. The ending changes the meaning.

---

## Theme 26「In order to ~ (purpose)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "in order to ~" and "for ~".

### 1. Two patterns
| Pattern | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ① verb | root + **नको लागि** (-nako lāgi) | पढ्नको लागि (in order to study) |
| ② noun | noun + **को लागि** (ko lāgi) | स्वास्थ्यको लागि (for health) |

### 2. Examples
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **पढ्नको लागि**, पुस्तकालय जान्छु। | paḍhnako lāgi, pustakālaya jānchu | To study, I go to the library |
| **स्वास्थ्यको लागि**, हरेक दिन व्यायाम गर्छु। | svāsthyako lāgi, harek din vyāyām garchu | For my health, I exercise every day |
| साथीलाई **भेट्नको लागि**, स्टेशनसम्म गएँ। | sāthīlāī bheṭnako lāgi, sṭeśansamma gaẽ | To meet my friend, I went to the station |

⚠️ Verbs take **नको लागि**, nouns take **को लागि**. Put the purpose part first.

---

## Theme 27「Conditionals (if ~)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "if ~, then …".

### 1. Formula: **यदि (yadi) ~ भने (bhane)**, …
Start with यदि (if), and put **भने** (then/if) after the condition.
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **यदि** पानी पर्यो **भने**, छाता लिएर जान्छु। | yadi pānī paryo bhane, chātā lier jānchu | If it rains, I'll take an umbrella |
| **यदि** फुर्सद छ **भने**, आउनुस्। | yadi phursad cha bhane, āunus | If you're free, please come |
| **यदि** सस्तो छ **भने**, किन्छु। | yadi sasto cha bhane, kinchu | If it's cheap, I'll buy it |
| **यदि** उ आउँछ **भने**, म पनि जान्छु। | yadi u āũcha bhane, ma pani jānchu | If he comes, I'll go too |

⚠️ **यदि** can be dropped, but keep **भने**. "condition + भने" = "if ~".

---

## Theme 28「Passive (~ was done)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "~ is/was done".

### 1. Formula: root + **इयो (-iyo)** (was ~ed)
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ढोका **खोलियो**। | ḍhokā kholiyo | The door was opened |
| चिठी **लेखियो**। | ciṭhī lekhiyo | The letter was written |
| झ्याल **फुटाइयो**。 | jhyāl phuṭāiyo | The window was broken |
| त्यो फिल्म गत वर्ष **बनाइयो**। | tyo philm gat varṣa banāiyo | That film was made last year |

### 2. "read by ~"
यो किताब धेरै मानिसहरूले **पढिन्छ**। (yo kitāb dherai mānisharūle paḍhincha = This book is read by many people)

⚠️ "was ~ed" = root + **इयो**. To name the doer, use **~ले (by ~)**.

---

## Theme 29「Causative (make someone ~)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "make/have (someone) ~".

### 1. Two ways
| Way | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ① special verb | feed = **खुवाउनु**, have-read = **पढाउनु**, put-to-sleep = **सुताउनु** | — |
| ② make ~ | root + **न लगाउनु** (-na lagāunu) | गर्न लगाउनु (make do) |

### 2. The person made to do it takes **~लाई**; the subject takes **~ले**
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| आमाले बच्चालाई औषधि **खुवाउनुहुन्छ**। | āmāle baccālāī auṣadhi khuvāunuhuncha | Mother feeds the child medicine |
| शिक्षकले विद्यार्थीलाई किताब **पढाउनुहुन्छ**। | śikṣakle vidyārthīlāī kitāb paḍhāunuhuncha | The teacher has the students read the book |
| बुबाले मलाई कार **चलाउन लगाउनुभयो**। | bubāle malāī kār calāuna lagāunubhayo | Father made me drive the car |
| बुबाले मलाई स्कूल **पठाउनुभयो**। | bubāle malāī skūl paṭhāunubhayo | Father sent me to school |

⚠️ "the one who makes" = subject + **ले**; "the one made to do it" = **~लाई**. Keep both.

---

## Theme 30「Reported Speech (said that ~)」

🎯 **Goal**: Say "(someone) said that ~".

### 1. Formula: (the quoted content) + **भन्यो／भनिन्／भन्नुभयो** (said)
| Speaker | "said" | Romanized |
|---|---|---|
| he | भन्यो | bhanyo |
| she | भनिन् | bhanin |
| polite | भन्नुभयो | bhannubhayo |

### 2. Examples (content first, "said" last)
| Sentence | Romanized | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| उसले **आउँछु भन्यो**। | usle āũchu bhanyo | He said he will come |
| उसले **जाँदिनँ भनिन्**। | usle jā̃dinã bhanin | She said she won't go |
| शिक्षकले **भोलि परीक्षा छ भन्नुभयो**। | śikṣakle bholi parīkṣā cha bhannubhayo | The teacher said there is an exam tomorrow |
| आमाले **चाँडो सुत भन्नुभयो**。 | āmāle cā̃ḍo sut bhannubhayo | Mother said to go to sleep early |

⚠️ Nepali has no separate word for "that"; you **place the quote directly and put भन्यो at the end**. The order matches the idea → "said".

---

## Summary (the key hurdles)
1. **ले (Themes 3, 17, 21)** … attaches to the subject of transitive verbs. The most important point.
2. **छ vs हो (Themes 1, 13)** … actions/qualities use छ, "noun = noun" uses हो.
3. **Endings change by gender/politeness (Themes 1, 8, 11)** … -छु/-छ/-छिन्, -को/-की.
4. **End-of-sentence markers (19 जस्तो लाग्छ / 30 भन्यो)** … the idea comes first, the keyword last.
